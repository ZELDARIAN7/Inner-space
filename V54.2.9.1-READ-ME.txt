INNER SPACE V54.2.9.1 — MEDITATE QA PASS

Built directly from V54.2.9.

Targeted fixes only:
- 3-Minute Reset now defaults to 3 minutes.
- Repeat Intentionally always restarts from 0:00 instead of inheriting a resumed timestamp.
- Pause cannot allow a still-loading ElevenLabs line to start speaking while the session is paused; if narration was interrupted, that cue is replayed on resume.
- Walking Presence uses walking-specific cues instead of stationary focus cues and includes route/traffic/terrain awareness.
- Added gentle opt-out/safety language: eyes may stay open; users can return to feet/sounds/room or stop if inward attention is uncomfortable.
- Sleep sessions carry a safe-rest/not-driving note and use softer music levels.
- Body/open-awareness cue banks include grounding alternatives.
- Reworded a few potentially pressuring prompts and removed the clinical-sounding “settle the nervous system” / “somatic release” labels.
- Removed Body Scan from the Sleep shelf because its ending is a return-to-alertness body scan, not a sleep-specific ending.
- “For You Today” renamed “Today’s Practice”; it is a daily rotation, not personalization.

No Room, Tone, Adept, Before I Go, Decide, Create, or other app systems were redesigned in this pass.
