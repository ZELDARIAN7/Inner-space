INNER SPACE — THE VOICE — RECORDING LIST

EASIEST OF ALL: open http://localhost:3000/studio.html while the app runs. Every line below is a
button there; it saves into this folder by itself. This list is only for recording another way.

Record 2 files per sound and drop them in this folder (assets/guides/voice/).
The app checks this folder on every open: a sound that has its files uses YOUR voice,
a sound that doesn't yet uses the device voice. So you can add them one at a time.

File 1  v_<id>_say.mp3    You say the name, then the one-line instruction below. 8–15 seconds.
File 2  v_<id>_sound.mp3  You make the sound itself, the way you'd want someone to copy it. 10–15 seconds.

EASIEST: record on your phone's voice recorder, any format, and send the files to Claude in the chat.
Claude cleans them (noise, hum, room), makes your voice warmer and fuller, gives the chant sounds a
stone-room depth, names them correctly and puts them in this folder for you.
Raw is fine — say a name first, e.g. "Ram, sound", then the sound. One file per sound is enough.

(until you do, the device voice says them)

v_line_listen.mp3  →  "Listen."
v_line_yours.mp3   →  "Your turn."
v_line_again.mp3   →  "Again. Longer this time."
v_line_settle.mp3  →  "Let the sound sit where it lives. Once more."
v_line_last.mp3    →  "The last one. Let it fade on its own."
v_line_close.mp3   →  "Breathe normally. Notice the place. That is what the sound does — it shows you where you are."

The one line that is different for each sound ("This is Ram. It lives in the stomach, just under the ribs.")
is spoken by the device voice for now; if you want it in your voice too, say so and I'll add v_<id>_place.mp3 slots.

01. The Hum   (Mmm)
    v_hum_say.mp3    →  "The Hum. Lips closed, teeth apart, jaw soft. Breathe in through the nose, then hum the whole breath out. Feel it in the lips and nose."
    v_hum_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Mmm

02. Om   (Om)
    v_om_say.mp3    →  "Om. Three parts in one breath: “Aaa” open in the belly, “Uuu” rounding in the chest, “Mmm” closing in the head. Let the M be the longest."
    v_om_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Om

03. Hu   (Hu)
    v_hu_say.mp3    →  "Hu. A long exhale shaped as “Huuu”, almost a sigh with a sound in it. Chest open, eyes closed, let the H carry air and the U carry tone."
    v_hu_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Hu

04. Ah   (Ah)
    v_ah_say.mp3    →  "Ah. Jaw drops, tongue flat, throat open like a yawn. One long “Aaah” on one note. Do not push; let it fall out."
    v_ah_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Ah

05. Lam   (Lam)
    v_lam_say.mp3    →  "Lam. Short L, then the “am” hummed down into the pelvis. Low pitch. Feel it in the seat."
    v_lam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Lam

06. Vam   (Vam)
    v_vam_say.mp3    →  "Vam. Teeth touch the lower lip for the V, then open into “am”. Feel the buzz move from lip to belly."
    v_vam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Vam

07. Ram   (Ram)
    v_ram_say.mp3    →  "Ram. A rolled or soft R, then “am” with the belly firm. The brightest of the seeds; let it have an edge."
    v_ram_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Ram

08. Yam   (Yam)
    v_yam_say.mp3    →  "Yam. A soft Y, then “am” sounded in the middle of the chest. Hand on the sternum to feel it."
    v_yam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Yam

09. Ham   (Ham)
    v_ham_say.mp3    →  "Ham. Breathy H into “am”, pitched a little higher, felt at the throat. Keep the neck long."
    v_ham_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Ham

10. So Hum   (So · Hum)
    v_sohum_say.mp3    →  "So Hum. Think “Sooo” on the inhale, sound “Hummm” on the exhale. The in-breath is silent; only the out-breath is heard."
    v_sohum_sound.mp3  →  the sound: So · Hum

11. Sat Nam   (Sat · Nam)
    v_satnam_say.mp3    →  "Sat Nam. “Sat” short and clipped like a drum hit, “Naaam” long and open. Often done on the exhale, repeated."
    v_satnam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Sat · Nam

12. Amen   (Amen)
    v_amen_say.mp3    →  "Amen. Open “Aaa”, then close slowly onto the “mmm” and let the N hum out the nose. Treat it as Om with a door at the end."
    v_amen_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Amen

13. Salam · Shalom   (Sa · laam)
    v_salam_say.mp3    →  "Salam · Shalom. “Sa” light, “laaam” long with the tongue resting behind the teeth, the M closing softly. Direct it at someone, even absent."
    v_salam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Sa · laam

14. Alleluia   (Al · le · lu · ia)
    v_alleluia_say.mp3    →  "Alleluia. Four syllables, each on its own pitch, the last “ia” held longest. Sing it, do not say it. Badly is fine."
    v_alleluia_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Al · le · lu · ia

15. Abracadabra   (A · bra · ca · da · bra)
    v_abracadabra_say.mp3    →  "Abracadabra. Five syllables, one breath, said slower each round until the last is a whisper. Let the word shrink the way the triangle did."
    v_abracadabra_sound.mp3  →  the sound: A · bra · ca · da · bra

16. Shazam   (Sha · zam)
    v_shazam_say.mp3    →  "Shazam. One short loud burst. Stand up for it. The “sh” is the inhale held, the “zam” is everything let go at once."
    v_shazam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Sha · zam

17. Open Sesame   (O · pen · se · sa · me)
    v_opensesame_say.mp3    →  "Open Sesame. Say it to a closed door in your life. Slowly, once, like you mean it. Then open the door."
    v_opensesame_sound.mp3  →  the sound: O · pen · se · sa · me

18. Ma   (Ma)
    v_ma_say.mp3    →  "Ma. Lips together on the M, then fall open into a soft “aaa”. Low, warm, lazy. Repeat it like a lullaby to yourself."
    v_ma_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Ma

19. Nam Myoho Renge Kyo   (Nam · Myo · ho · Ren · ge · Kyo)
    v_nam_say.mp3    →  "Nam Myoho Renge Kyo. Fast, even, like a train on tracks — about two seconds per round. Do not sing it; drive it."
    v_nam_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Nam · Myo · ho · Ren · ge · Kyo

20. Om Mani Padme Hum   (Om · Ma · ni · Pad · me · Hum)
    v_om_mani_say.mp3    →  "Om Mani Padme Hum. Six syllables, low and even, the “Hum” dropping lowest. Slow enough to feel each one; a round every four or five seconds."
    v_om_mani_sound.mp3  →  the sound: Om · Ma · ni · Pad · me · Hum
