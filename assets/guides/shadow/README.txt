THE SHADOW — meditation recordings (optional)

The five meditations play with the device's own voice, slowly, with long silences.
If you record one yourself (or with ElevenLabs), drop the file here and it plays instead:

  m_meet.mp3    Meet the part             (9 min)
  m_child.mp3   The one who was sent away  (9 min)
  m_guard.mp3   Thank the guard            (8 min)
  m_lake.mp3    The lake                   (8 min)
  m_night.mp3   Gather them for sleep      (10 min)

Record the whole meditation as one file, silences included. .m4a / .webm / .ogg also work.
The script for each is inside the app (open it once and read along), or ask Claude for the text.
