NEW DAILY RETURN FEATURES
1) TODAY'S DOOR
- A two-minute Room micro-practice that changes every calendar day.
- 12 original Doors rotate day by day: listening, vision, touch, effort release, depth, colour, ordinary appreciation, stillness, wide vision, a clear next move, temperature, and more.
- No score and no artificial penalty for missing a day.
- Completing the Door once that day adds a small light to Your Space.
- Home shows a gentle day rhythm when several consecutive Doors were completed.
- A completed Door can be replayed without adding duplicate daily lights.

2) TOMORROW NOTE
- Leave one short line for tomorrow.
- It stays sealed today and becomes a reveal card when the Room is opened on or after the due day.
- After reveal, the user can keep it in Saved Moments or write the next note.
- Stored only in this browser/local storage and explicitly described as not encrypted.
