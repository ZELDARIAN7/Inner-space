Voice Studio

Record the Voice in your own voice. Tap Record, speak or sound, tap Stop. It saves straight into the app — the next time you open a session, it plays you.

How to sound good: quiet room, door closed, phone or headset mic a hand's width from your mouth. Leave one second of silence before you start and after you finish. For the sounds, take a full breath and hold one note for 8–12 seconds — the session loops it. Redo as many times as you like; only the last take is kept. After you're done, send the folder assets/guides/voice to Claude and it comes back cleaned, warmer and fuller.
