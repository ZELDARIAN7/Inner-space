DATA AND LIMITS
New journal entries save only when explicitly requested, in this browser's
local storage. They are not encrypted; shared-device users can access them.
The latest 100 entries are retained. Clearing browser data removes them.
Drawing is session-only unless exported. Undo retains up to 15 snapshots.
New guided prompts are English. Device speech availability depends on the
browser/OS and installed voices. Visual guidance does not require voice.
Keep the app open for timed rest and walk cues. A sleeping or locked device
may delay a web timer; use a separate alarm when exact waking time matters.
No new AI/API service is required for these Room modes.
Existing AI-backed areas still use the original server configuration.
