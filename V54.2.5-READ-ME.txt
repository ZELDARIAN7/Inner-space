INNER SPACE V54.2.5

Added two flagship Room modes:

BEFORE I GO
- Meeting, presentation, difficult talk, date/social, gym, work, custom.
- 2 / 4 / 7 minute guided launch.
- Brian-compatible narration with race-safe Next cue and voice toggle.
- Procedural threshold/portal visuals that brighten through the session.
- Situation-specific carry line and GO ending.
- Remembers the last use and asks for a lightweight outcome check on the next visit.

DECIDE
- Two or three user-written options.
- Fast / Deeper / Full reflection depth.
- Interactive decision chamber with animated paths, fog, tap-reactive light, and Brian guidance.
- Uses only the user's own responses to show a lean; explicitly not a verdict.
- Captures one next useful move and can save it to Saved Moments.

Existing Room modes and V54.2.4 Create/Clear Your Head fixes are preserved.
API keys are not bundled. Keep server/.env private.
